# Rotember 2026 — race day recap (mind dump, Sep 27)

Raw dump captured the night of race day, organized into buckets. The
voice is Mikian's; the structure is just so it's findable later. Open
questions at the bottom are where the memo cut off — pick them up when
the adrenaline wears off.

## The row

| | |
|---|---|
| Distance | 5K on the erg |
| Time | **17:52** |
| Previous best | ~18:19 |
| PR margin | **27 seconds** |
| Setting | Live, in front of a crowd, at an event I hosted |

**What made it happen**
- I seeded myself **between AJ and Adam**. I knew before the countdown
  that this was the most important decision of the night, and it was.
  Rowing in front of those two the whole way was the whole race.
- Brandy (I think) counted us down.
- The crowd. See the leaderboard section — nobody rowed alone.

**What I got away with**
- **No real warm-up.** A few strokes back and forth, hands under the
  knees, a little stretch. That's roughly how I always warm up, but for a
  PR attempt I should have gotten the muscles genuinely warm and
  stretched properly. Note for next race day: build a 10–15 minute
  warm-up into the schedule so racers don't get on cold.

**How the goal evolved**
1. Rowed a 10K under 40 minutes, asked "what's the next step?"
2. Not 100K in one sitting. **100K in a month.** That was the original
   Rotember goal, and where the row100k name comes from.
3. Somewhere in there it became "get other people in" — probably because
   I knew I could build a leaderboard, and I knew the thing was
   *possible*, so it would be easy for people to join.
4. Personal goal climbed to 500K at sub-2:00 and stuck there.

**Data I wish I had**

## Race day

**Turnout:** 35–40 people, ~20 racers, the rest spectators.

**What I put together**
- Free shirts for everyone. Everyone wore them.
- A sponsor. I asked for the sponsorship and got it.
- Free stuff from Lululemon. Same: I asked.
- Hired a photographer.
- The live leaderboard on the TV.

**The leaderboard was the event.** Every connected rower on screen with
meters remaining and, most importantly, **who finishes next**. The
crowd self-organized around it: someone finishes, everyone cheers them
in, looks up, sees who's next, and moves. Nobody had to think about it.
Almost nobody got skipped, and even when the main group was on one
rower there were still two or three people cheering the other one.

It could be a lot better technically, and it didn't matter. The
"who's next" affordance is the feature. Protect it in any redesign.

**Merch:** the shirts worked, but next year I pay someone who's good at
this to design the merch.

## Where the head is at

Honest read, same night:

  sure I'd want it even if they could.
- **Monthly:** maybe. That's the interesting cadence.
- **October:** too soon. Definitely not.

  `dev:row100k:dec` script pointed at Dec 15, so the idea has been
  floating around.)

Money: I'm going to put real money into events over the next few
months and years. Not a commitment, a direction. Possible sustainable
shapes:
- Sell merch.
- Make the monthly race day a paid event.

## Open questions (where the memo cut off)

The dump ended mid-sentence: *"Could that event be a paid monthly
event? Here's the thing, right? Like I have a couple..."*

Pick these up:

1. **What was the "couple" of things?** Constraints? Ideas? Objections
   to charging? Finish that sentence first.
2. **Paid vs. free.** Tonight was free with sponsor money and free
   gear. Does charging change who shows up, or the energy in the room?
   Could the sponsor cover it so it stays free for racers?
3. **What's the minimum viable race day?** Tonight had shirts, sponsor,
   photographer, leaderboard, Lululemon swag. Which of those made the
   night, and which were nice-to-have? Weekly is off the table partly
   because of this list.
4. **Is the monthly thing Rotember-shaped (a month of meters ending in
   a race) or just a race night?** Different amounts of work, different
   commitment from rowers.
5. **December scope.** If it's a race day and not a full month, what's
   the on-ramp for people who didn't do Rotember?
6. **Conversion.** Sign-up to first row, first row to finish, finisher
   to race-day attendee. Instrument all three before the next edition.
7. **Warm-up protocol** for race day. Schedule it, don't leave it to
   each rower.
