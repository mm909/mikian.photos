# The routes (researched 2026-11-16)

Owner, 2026-11-16: "We want to take the powerline route." `waypoints.json`
holds three: `powerline` (the default, 302 mi), `og` (338 mi) and `pahrump`
(the OG stretched through Shoshone and Pahrump, about 350 mi; its Pahrump →
NV-160 stretch is the router's pick and not checked). What follows is the
research as first written, when the OG line was the recommendation.

The course in `waypoints.json` is the "OG line": Santa Monica → Sunset →
Hollywood → North Hollywood → Sylmar → Sierra Hwy → Soledad Canyon → Acton →
Palmdale → El Mirage → Adelanto → Helendale → Route 66 → Barstow → Yermo →
Field Rd → (35 mi of dirt and sand beside I-15) → Baker → CA-127 → Tecopa →
Old Spanish Trail Hwy → NV-160 over Mountain Springs → Blue Diamond Rd →
Las Vegas Blvd → the sign. Built here: 338 mi, about 12,000 ft of climb.

Picked because it is the only line two ordinary vans can support the whole
way: after Baker every mile is paved, and before it I-15 exits meet the
dirt every 5–10 miles.

THE ALTERNATIVE is Powerline Rd: same to Baker, CA-127 north 9 mi, then
about 45 mi of transmission-line service road to Primm, a track beside I-15,
and Las Vegas Blvd from Jean. About 304 mi (36 shorter) and what the record
teams run, but roughly 53 mi with no van (reports disagree on whether even a
4x4 gets through), around 100°F in past years. Worth it with a
high-clearance truck and bikes; not with two rental vans. Its waypoints
after Baker (lat, lon):

Also weighed: Cajon Pass instead of the Antelope Valley (10 mi shorter to
Barstow, 1,800 ft more climb, 11 mi more dirt), and Shoshone / Pahrump
(longer; not on the OG line).

The three checkpoints are STAND-INS on the corridor (Elmer's Bottle Tree
Ranch, the Baker thermometer, Mountain Springs Saloon). Real secrets
checkpoints have been well off the direct line (Pioneertown, Amboy Crater in
2025), so expect to re-route when they are announced.

Not ground-truthed: distances, climb and dirt come from OpenStreetMap and
SRTM; services and cell coverage notes are from race reports and memory.
Sources: Red Bull's Red Bulletin piece on the race, Camino Ultra's 2024 solo
report, Cresting the Summit's 2023 report, Lim Chay Seng's 2022 recap, Team
Vaga's journal, InsideHook on Team Satisfy, Running Club Catalans' 2025
checkpoint report.
